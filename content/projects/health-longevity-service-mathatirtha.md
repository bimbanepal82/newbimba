The second initiative developed from BIMBA Nepal's interest in **health, longevity and dignified ageing**.

The **Pilot Project on Health Longevity Service** was conducted at **Mathatirtha Health Post, Chandragiri Municipality–8**.

The program particularly focused on people aged 60 and above while also extending relevant services to women and other members of the surrounding community.

The objective was not simply to provide medical consultation, but to explore how community-based services could support people in maintaining their health and identifying potential problems earlier.

## Services included

- Geriatric health assessment
- Geriatric consultation
- Physician consultation
- Blood pressure testing
- Blood sugar testing
- Video X-ray
- Gynecology consultation
- Women's health services
- Pulmonary Function Test
- Respiratory assessment
- Specialist consultation
- Medicines and health materials

Consultants from relevant medical fields, including **Radiology, Internal Medicine/Geriatrics and Gynecology**, participated in the program.

With support from **Cipla Nepal**, Pulmonary Function Testing and related consultation were also made available.

## Early identification matters

During the program, an acute health condition was identified in one participant. The individual was referred immediately to a hospital for further treatment.

This experience reinforced an important aspect of community health: **early identification can create an opportunity for timely action.** A community health program can therefore serve not only as a place for consultation, but also as a bridge between people and the healthcare system.

## Building the idea of Health Longevity

The Health Longevity Service pilot reflects BIMBA Nepal's belief that living longer should be accompanied by better health and dignity.

The focus is therefore not simply "How long can we live?" but also "How well can we live those years?"

This is where the concept of **स्वाभिमानी बुढ्यौली — Dignified Ageing** becomes central to BIMBA Nepal's work.
